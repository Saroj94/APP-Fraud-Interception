# APP-Fraud-Interception
APP Fraud Interception and Reimbursement-Priced Decisioning.

**The pain point**
Since the Payment Systems Regulator made reimbursement mandatory in October 2024, an APP scam is no longer only the customer’s loss — it is a bill the sending and receiving banks split between them. That single change converted fraud detection from a customer-service problem into a directly quantifiable P&L line, and it is the reason fraud analytics headcount in UK retail banking has grown while other analytics teams have not.
The uncomfortable part is that the fraud is authorised. The customer presses "send". Detection therefore cannot rely on the device-and-transaction signals that catch unauthorised card fraud; it has to infer that a genuine customer is being manipulated in real time. UK Finance made exactly this point in July 2026 — that fraud is "no longer solely a device or transaction-monitoring challenge but increasingly a customer protection one".
